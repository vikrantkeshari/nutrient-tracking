# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, particularly with people who are new to the codebase or to open source
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — critiquing the code, not the person
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users, not just for ourselves
- Assuming good faith when a message is short or a review sounds blunt; not everyone writes in their first language

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, for instance — without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissiveness toward beginners' questions
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages — and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through a GitHub direct message or the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Enforcement guidelines

| Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Correction** — inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour | A private written warning explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be requested |
| **Warning** — a single incident or a series of actions | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with those involved for a specified period |
| **Temporary ban** — a serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behaviour | A temporary ban from any interaction with the project |
| **Permanent ban** — a pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or classes of individuals | A permanent ban from any public interaction with the project |
